Our expertise in market analysis, cost-benefit assessment, risk modeling, and institutional capacity evaluation provided a comprehensive strategic roadmap for education sector investments, ensuring alignment with national development priorities and ADB guidelines to drive impactful, future-ready education.

Strategic Economic and Financial Advisory for Payra Seaport Development (2017)

IP3 Consulting provided technical expertise in economic and financial feasibility analysis for Payra Seaport, Bangladesh's third seaport, under a detailed techno-economic study and master planning initiative. 2019

Comprehensive Feasibility Study for Jolshiri Aqua Green City (2019)

IP3 Consulting conducted an economic, social, financial, and market feasibility study for Jolshiri Aqua Green City, a major Army Officers Housing Scheme development project in Bangladesh. Our work included a 10-year projection, cost-benefit analyses, financial modeling, and market segmentation, providing investment strategies, pricing models, and funding opportunities to ensure the project's long-term viability and sustainable urban growth.

Strategic Advisory for Equity Investment & Special Economic Zone Development (2017)

Evaluation of World Bank's ESMAP & ASTAE Programs (2016)
