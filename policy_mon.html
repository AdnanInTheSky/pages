Track Record

MERLA Projects

2019

Health Access and Linkage Opportunities for Workers [HALOW+] (2019)

IP3 Consulting played a key role in the HALOW+ Program, a multi-stakeholder initiative led by Marks & Spencer (M&S), GlaxoSmithKline (GSK), PwC-UK, Care International, and Tufts University-USA to improve health service markets, worker well-being, and factory productivity in Bangladesh's ready-made garment (RMG) sector.

2020

Transforming Urban Sanitation with Data-Driven Monitoring & Evaluation (2020)

IP3 Consulting designed the Monitoring & Evaluation (M&E) and Results Framework for the Dhaka Sewerage Improvement Project (DSIP), a five-year initiative aimed at enhancing sewerage networks, wastewater treatment, and non-network sanitation services.

2018

Accelerating Clean Energy Access Through Results-Based Financing (2018)

IP3 Consulting led the design and implementation of a results-based financing strategy for the Global Lighting & Energy Access Partnership (Global LEAP), fostering self-sustaining commercial markets for off-grid solar solutions.
